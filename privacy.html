The short version

Queen’s Arrow Escape (the “Game”) is a puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web, made by AlphaYashInteractive (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the Game stores and shares, why, and what you can do about it. Signing in is optional and is used only to back up your progress. The Game has no chat and no user-generated content.

  • We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or precise location, and you never have to create an account to play.
  • Your progress, coins, hints and settings are saved on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your progress (not your settings) is also saved in the cloud, so you can get it back on a new phone.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. You choose whether ads are personalised.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see your payment details.
  • In this version no analytics data is sent to us or to any third party.
  • You can erase the game data on your device at any time (Settings > Delete my data), and delete your account and cloud save (Settings > Save your progress > Delete account and cloud save).

3. Information collected by services the Game uses

Ads (Google AdMob). The Game shows full-screen ads between levels and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for a reward (extra hints or coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. According to Google’s published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • IP address (used to estimate the device’s general location);
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID (on iOS, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked);
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Google uses this to select, deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep its service working. The data is encrypted in transit. By default the Game marks ad requests as non-personalised. If you turn on personalised ads in Settings > Privacy, Google may use the data above to tailor ads to your interests. If you leave it off, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form (a certified consent management platform) is shown where the law requires, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy options. On iOS the system asks for permission before the advertising identifier is used.

The “Remove ads” purchase stops the full-screen ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay available because you choose to watch them. See How Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play and Apple). Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed (the product id) so we can unlock it. We never receive your card number, billing name or address.

Analytics. The Game contains a small event system that records anonymous gameplay events (such as “level started” or “hint used”) in memory on your device. In this version these events are not sent to us or to any third party. If a future version sends them, we will update this policy first and the “Anonymous stats” switch in Settings will control it.

Sign-in and cloud save (Google Firebase). Signing in is optional. If you choose to continue with Google in Settings, the Game uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services run by Google, to identify your account and to keep a copy of your progress. This is what is stored:

  • From the sign-in service: a random account ID, which provider you used, and the profile details that provider shares with the Game, usually your email address, display name and a link to your profile picture. The Game shows your email address or name in Settings so you can see which account is signed in. We use these details only to run your account.
  • In the cloud database: one record per account with your progress (levels completed with stars and best results, coins, hints, owned and equipped items, whether “Remove ads” was bought and the purchase ids, daily and login streaks, achievements and play counters), a revision number, the time of the last save, and the random install ID of the phone that saved it. Your settings, ad choices and consent are not uploaded.
  • The database is in the Google Cloud region asia-south1. Google encrypts the data in transit and at rest. Access rules let only the signed-in account read or change its own record.
  • Google, as our service provider, also handles technical data such as your IP address and sign-in times to run and secure these services.

Google sign-in. When you sign in, the provider learns that you signed in to this Game and handles that under its own privacy policy (Google). If you do not sign in, none of this happens and nothing leaves your device for this feature.

Store reports. Google Play and the Apple App Store give us aggregated information about installs, ratings, crashes and performance. We cannot identify you from it.

4. What the Game does not collect

  • No phone number, postal address, date of birth or government ID, and no account, name or email address unless you choose to sign in (section 3).
  • No precise or approximate location from GPS or Wi-Fi (Google may estimate a general location from your IP address for ads).
  • No contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera, microphone, messages or health data.
  • No user-generated content, chat or social features.

Android permissions the Game requests: INTERNET (to load ads, process purchases and (if enabled) send analytics); ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (so ads and purchases know whether you are online); com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (so Google AdMob can read the Android advertising ID); VIBRATE (for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings). You can switch off the advertising ID in your device’s privacy settings.

7. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: Settings > Privacy > Personalised ads (off by default).
  • Ad privacy options: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Settings > Ad privacy options reopens Google’s consent form.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID (the path varies by device); on iOS, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Cloud save: Settings > Save your progress. Sign in or out whenever you like; signing out keeps your progress on the phone. “Delete account and cloud save” removes the cloud copy and your account (section 10).
  • Delete game data: Settings > Delete my data (section 10).

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In some countries the minimum age to consent to data processing is higher (up to 16 in parts of the EEA); if that applies to you, please use the Game only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact alphayashinteractive@gmail.com and we will delete it. Parents can also use the family controls on Google Play and Apple to limit ads and purchases.

9. How long we keep information

Game data stays on your device until you delete it, reset the Game or uninstall it. If you sign in, the cloud copy of your progress and your account are kept until you delete your account (Settings > Save your progress > Delete account and cloud save) or ask us to (section 10). Logs and backups that Google keeps are removed on Google’s normal schedule. If you never sign in, we keep no copy of your game data on our servers. Google and Apple keep ad and purchase data under their own policies and retention periods.

10. Deleting your data

Use Settings > Delete my data to erase the progress, coins, items, statistics and settings saved on your device and to generate a new random install ID. Uninstalling the Game also removes its local data (device backups, if you use them, are managed by your device and can be deleted in your Google or Apple account settings). Purchases are tied to your Google or Apple account, not to the Game’s save file, and can be restored with “Restore purchases”; deleting the game data does not refund them.

Delete your account and cloud save: Settings > Save your progress > Delete account and cloud save. This permanently deletes the cloud copy of your progress and your account in our sign-in service. The game saved on your phone is not changed (use “Delete my data” for that). Your Google account itself is not affected; manage it in that provider’s settings.

If the Game is no longer installed, you can ask us to delete your account and cloud save at https://alphayashinteractive.github.io/delete-account.html or by email to alphayashinteractive@gmail.com, from the address you used to sign in (or tell us which provider and display name you used). We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days.

If you never signed in, we do not hold your game data and cannot delete it for you remotely. To delete data held by Google (for example your advertising profile), use Google’s account tools at myaccount.google.com or your device’s ad settings.

11. Security

The Game keeps its data in app-private storage protected by your device’s operating system, and its network connections use encryption (TLS). Cloud saves are protected by your sign-in and by database rules that let only your own account read or change its record. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International transfers

Google, Apple and any analytics provider (and, for cloud save, Google’s Firebase services) process data in several countries, including the United States. Where required, they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. Details are in their privacy policies.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, other US state laws, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, to withdraw consent at any time, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to complain to your data protection authority.

If you use cloud save, we can find your account from the email address or display name you signed in with, and you can see and delete that data yourself in the Game (sections 7 and 10). If you never sign in, because the Game has no account, we usually cannot link a request to a particular device, and most data is already under your control on your device (sections 7 and 10). For data held by Google or Apple, please use their tools. For anything else, write to alphayashinteractive@gmail.com; we will answer within the time the law requires, and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. Grievance contact: alphayashinteractive@gmail.com.
