1. Delete it inside the Game (fastest)

  • Open the Game and tap the gear (Settings) on the home screen.
  • Find “Save your progress”, then tap “Delete account and cloud save”.
  • Confirm with “DELETE”. If the sign-in service asks you to sign in once more to confirm that it is really you, do so.

That is all. Your account and your cloud save are deleted straight away.

2. What is deleted and what is not

Deleted: your account in our sign-in service (account ID, and the email address, display name and profile picture link that Google shared with the Game) and your cloud save (levels, stars, coins, hints, items, streaks, achievements and counters).

Not deleted: the game saved on your phone (use Settings > Delete my data for that, or uninstall the Game); purchases, which belong to your Google Play or Apple account and can be restored later; your Google account itself; and ad data that Google holds under its own policy.

3. If the Game is no longer installed

  • Reinstall the Game, sign in with the same account and follow section 1; or
  • email alphayashinteractive@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in, with the subject “Delete my account”, and say which provider you used (Google). If your account has no email address, tell us the provider, your display name and roughly when you first signed in.

We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done. Copies in logs or backups that Google keeps are removed on Google’s normal schedule.
